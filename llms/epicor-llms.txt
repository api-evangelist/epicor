# Epicor | ERP Software Solutions for Industry Leaders

> Advanced ERP software curated to the unique needs of businesses in manufacturing, distribution, retail, and building supply.

Epicor provides industry-specific tools designed for quicker ROI, better efficiency, and streamlined growth. Serving over 23,000 customers in 150+ countries, Epicor focuses on the "Make, Move, Sell" framework to optimize the global supply chain.

# Epicor

> Industry-specific ERP software for manufacturers, distributors, and retailers.

Epicor provides enterprise resource planning (ERP) software solutions tailored to the specific needs of various industries, including manufacturing, distribution, building supply, automotive, and retail. Their offerings aim to streamline operations, improve efficiency, accelerate return on investment, and foster business growth through industry expertise and advanced technology.

The website serves as a comprehensive resource for businesses seeking to understand and implement ERP solutions. It details their product suite, industry-specific applications, technological innovations like AI and cloud solutions, and customer success stories. Users can explore product features, learn about industry best practices, and find resources to support their business transformation.

When interacting with an LLM about Epicor, it's important to understand that their focus is on providing robust, industry-tailored ERP systems. The content covers a wide range of software modules and solutions, from core ERP functionalities to specialized tools for areas like supply chain management, financial planning, digital commerce, and connected worker solutions. The site also highlights their commitment to innovation, including AI-driven applications and cloud-based services.
